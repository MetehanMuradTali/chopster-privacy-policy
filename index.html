Google and its advertising partners may collect or process information such as device identifiers, IP address, advertising ID where available, app interactions, diagnostic information, and advertising-related data to provide, measure, and improve advertising services, subject to applicable settings and law.

Advertising personalization and data processing may depend on your device settings, consent choices, region, and Google's policies.

For more information about how Google processes data, please visit Google's Privacy Policy .

3. How We Use Information

Gameplay preferences stored locally are used to support the Game's features. Information processed by advertising providers is handled according to their applicable privacy policies and settings.

We do not operate our own user account system in the Game. Information may be processed by third-party service providers, including Google AdMob, as described in this policy.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored gameplay preferences remain on your device until they are overwritten or removed, for example by clearing the Game's app data or uninstalling the Game. Data handled by third parties is subject to their retention and deletion policies.

6. Children's Privacy

The Game's intended audience is determined by its distribution and target-audience settings. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us using the address below.
